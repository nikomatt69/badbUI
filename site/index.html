Honest limits

What BadbUI cannot do

We would rather tell you plainly than let you find out in a raid.

No arenas on Forever

WoW Forever has no arenas. The Arena module (/badb arena) is included and ready, but there is currently nothing for it to run in.

Addons can't read your GPU

SystemProfile (/badb system) reports what the game exposes. No addon can read your graphics card model or GPU load — anything claiming otherwise is guessing.

Quest coverage is partial

Questie-style coverage on Forever is partial: some quests, objectives and spawn points are missing or approximate. The tracker helps, but it is not complete.

Start with the wizard, change anything later

Install, type /badb in game, and the setup wizard builds your first layout. Every module, preset and keybind can be changed afterwards — nothing is locked in.
